Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

Vouze is a trip planning and expense splitting app. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. We keep it simple because we're a small indie app with no interest in monetizing your data.

What We Collect

  • Account info — your email address and display name when you sign up
  • Profile data — avatar, payment handles (Venmo, Cash App, Zelle) if you choose to add them
  • Trip data — trip names, locations, dates, itinerary items, expenses, and settlement records you create
  • Photos — images you upload to trips, stored securely in the cloud
  • Usage data — basic server logs including IP addresses and request metadata, collected passively by our hosting infrastructure

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and operate the Vouze service
  • To send you a welcome email when you create an account
  • To enable trip collaboration with people you invite
  • To improve the app based on usage patterns

We do not sell your data. We do not run ads. We do not use your data for any purpose beyond operating Vouze.

Third Parties

Your data passes through the following trusted services to operate Vouze:

  • Supabase — database and file storage (AWS us-east-1). Privacy policy →
  • Vercel — app hosting and delivery. Privacy policy →
  • Resend — transactional email (welcome email only). Privacy policy →
  • Google — if you choose to sign in with Google OAuth. Privacy policy →

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure hosted on AWS in the United States. We use row-level security policies to ensure users can only access data they are authorized to see. Photos are stored in secured cloud storage.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete a trip, it is soft-deleted and may be recoverable for a limited time. If you delete your account, your personal data will be permanently removed within 30 days.

Your Rights

  • Access — you can view all your data within the app
  • Deletion — you can delete trips, photos, and request full account deletion by emailing us
  • Portability — you can download your photos at any time from within the app
  • Correction — you can update your profile information at any time

Children

Vouze is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. We'll update the date at the top when we do. Continued use of Vouze after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact
